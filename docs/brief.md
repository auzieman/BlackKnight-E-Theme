# BlackKnight theme/tutorial kit — working name
Outcome: a blue-metal E desktop identity and an editable tutorial showing how its background and controls are assembled.
First implementation candidate: inspect Sci-Fi and Transient-Color sources, then make a still composition plus a representative border/menu preview. Animation follows visual acceptance.
Later lessons: EDC/EDJ build, layering, eye glow, fog/lightning, low-motion variant and packaging. Exact artwork and lesson sequence remain exploratory.
Acceptance for source study: identify source paths/revisions, relevant groups/signals, animation mechanism, license/provenance and E27 uncertainties. No claim of reuse or compatibility from screenshots alone.
Acceptance for a later visual slice: preview on laptop and external-display dimensions, readable controls and matching still/animated palette. Keep controls unaffected by full-scene flashes.
